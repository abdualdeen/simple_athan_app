# Simply Athan — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Simply Athan ("the app") is a free, open-source prayer times and Qibla compass
app. The short version of this policy: **the app has no servers, no accounts,
no advertising, no analytics, and no trackers — your data stays on your
device.**

## What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in your device's local app storage. This
information never leaves your device, and it is removed when you uninstall the
app or clear its data:

- Prayer time calculation settings (calculation method and Asr madhab).
- Locations you save (up to two, each a place name with its coordinates), used
  to show prayer times and the Qibla direction.
- Notification settings (whether reminders are enabled, which prayers are
  muted, and the selected athan sound).
- Display settings such as the app theme.
- Whether you have completed the app's introduction.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Location (while the app is in use)** — used to calculate prayer times for
  where you are and to point the Qibla compass. The app accesses your location
  only while it is open in the foreground. Your coordinates are kept on the
  device only if you choose to save a location; otherwise they are used in
  memory and discarded.
- **Notifications** — so the app can show prayer reminders.
- **Alarms and reminders (exact scheduling)** — so prayer reminders can be
  delivered at the exact prayer time, including after a device restart.
- **Internet access** — used only by your device's built-in geocoding service
  (see the next section). The app itself does not connect to any server.

## What the app never does

- It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
- It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking
  components.
- It does not create accounts and does not know who you are.
- It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or files.

## Place names and geocoding

To display a place name for your location, and to search for places by name,
the app uses the geocoding service built into your device's operating system.
Depending on your device, that service may be operated by the device
manufacturer or operating system vendor, and it may process the coordinates or
search text you provide according to that provider's own terms and privacy
policy. The app has no servers of its own and receives nothing from that
service beyond the place names it returns.

## Open source

The app's full source code is publicly available at
[github.com/abdualdeen/simple_athan_app](https://github.com/abdualdeen/simple_athan_app/)
so that anyone can verify the claims in this policy.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all data it has stored. You can also remove saved
locations within the app, or clear the app's data from your device settings at
any time.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling ever changes, an updated version of this policy
will be published in the app's repository and linked from the app's About
screen.
